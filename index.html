PickleWatch Privacy Statement

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Hey there, Pickleball Player!

I'm George Yameen, the creator of PickleWatch. I built this app because I love pickleball and wanted a better way to track my swings. Here's the straight talk on how your data is handled.

What PickleWatch Does & Doesn't Do

PickleWatch uses your Apple Watch sensors to track your pickleball swings and provide feedback. Your detailed swing data stays on your device. Only a short game summary is sent, see Analytics below. We collect anonymous usage analytics to improve the app.

Your Data Stays Put

PickleWatch processes your swing tracking locally on your Apple Watch and iPhone. Your swing stats, performance metrics, and training data stay on your devices. Anonymous usage data (screens viewed, button clicks, app interactions) and a short summary of each game are sent to Google Analytics to help us improve the app.

Analytics We Use

We use Google Analytics (by Firebase) to see how the app is used, like which screens get opened and how many games get played. When you finish a game, the app sends a short summary with the score, game length, swing counts and top speed. It never sends your name, your email or anything else that identifies you. Older versions also sent your average heart rate and calories with that summary, and that stops with the next update. Google also sees basic device info (phone model, iOS version) and a rough, city level location from your IP address. None of this is linked to you or used for ads.

We also use Smartlook, which records how people move through the app's screens, so we can find the confusing parts.

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report so we can fix it.

Kids Can Play Too

PickleWatch is safe for players of all ages. We don't collect any personal information that identifies users.
